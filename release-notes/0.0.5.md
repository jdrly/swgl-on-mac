## SWGL on Mac 0.0.5

A new Settings window with a sidebar.

### A new Settings window
- Settings (SWGL on Mac → Settings…, ⌘,) now has a sidebar: Graphics, Game, Mouse, Updates and Troubleshooting, each on its own page. It fits on smaller screens, and you can make it bigger or smaller.
- Show Metal HUD moved to the Game page. Shift-F9 hides or shows the HUD while you play, Shift-F10 switches its layout.
- Nothing about the settings themselves changed: your choices carry over.
