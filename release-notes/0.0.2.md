## SWGL on Mac 0.0.2

Coming from 0.0.1? Download this version once and drag it into Applications, replacing the old one. 0.0.1 can't update itself; from 0.0.2 on, updates come to you.

### Smooth mouse-look
- Turning the camera with the mouse is now smooth: no more judder that came and went with ⌘-Tab or after a stutter. The app now reads your mouse directly and passes every movement to the game right away.
- **New: Mouse-Look Speed** in Settings. 1× turns as fast as SWG on Windows; your macOS pointer speed no longer applies. If mouse-look feels slower than in 0.0.1, raise it or the in-game sensitivity (in testing, about 1.75× matched 0.0.1).

### Steadier frame rate
- After a hitch, the game no longer gets stuck at 30 fps for a second or two on 60 Hz displays.

### Settings and updates
- **SWGL on Mac → Settings… (⌘,)** replaces the Features menu: Retina Mode, Metal HUD, Game Mode, Mouse-Look Speed, Debug Mode and updates in one window.
- **Automatic updates:** from this version on, SWGL on Mac checks for new versions once a day (never while you're playing) and installs them when you click Install. You can turn the check off in Settings, or use **Check for Updates…** in the SWGL on Mac menu.
- The About panel and alerts now look like a current macOS app.

### Troubleshooting
- **Collect Logs…** in the SWGL on Mac menu saves a zip of the logs to your Desktop, with personal details left out, to send with a bug report.
- **Debug Mode** in Settings makes the logs much more detailed. Turn it on only when asked to.

### Support
SWGL on Mac is an unofficial project, not made, endorsed or supported by the SWG Legends team. Please send questions and bug reports to Pharaphobia on Discord or as a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jdrly/swgl-on-mac/issues), never to the SWG Legends team.
